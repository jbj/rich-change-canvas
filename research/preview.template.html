What changed after testing more projects?

Sampling limit: these are accepted low-discussion examples only. This study can reveal false alarms in proposed attention cues, but cannot estimate contentious-change recall, safe approval accuracy, or reviewer time saved.

Recommended product direction

Review desk for scanning many changes, one concise receipt for the decision, and commit zoom when the history needs explaining. Put architectural and complexity metrics behind drill-downs as their analyzers become available.

Measured versus deferred

Python has a scoped, real AST/function-decision-count probe. Other languages have file-level counts only. Call graphs, API compatibility, test coverage, and candidate ranking have not been computed here. Same-task alternatives remain a later goal.
